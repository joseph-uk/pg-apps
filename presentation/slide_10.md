*   **Value of Teamwork:** Forecasting is hard! Multiple eyes, different perspectives (weather nerds, local gurus, experienced XC pilots) improve decisions.
*   **Dales Groups (Telegram):**
    *   **Flying Plans:** General chat for all.
    *   **Progress:** Aimed at newer pilots focusing on site flying, ridge soaring, first thermals.
    *   **10/100:** Focus on XC - planning, flying together, retrieve. The primary group for XC discussions.
*   **Best Practice:**
    *   Do *your own* initial research (Windy!).
    *   Post *ideas* with screenshots & specific questions. "What do folk think of Stags Fell tomorrow based on this UKV? Looks marginal NE early?"
    *   If no replies, re-check basics (Rain? Too windy?). Maybe the idea needs refining.
*   **Outcome:** Better plans, shared understanding, flying buddies, safer flying.
