*   **What:** Civil Aircraft Notification Procedure.
*   **Purpose:** Notify primarily **midweek** air traffic (Military low flying, increasingly others via NOTAM) of planned gliding activity.
*   **Why:** Safety! Makes low-flying aircraft aware of your potential presence. Creates temporary advisory area (2nm radius, up to 2000ft AGL).
*   **How:**
    *   **Recommended:** Online/App (`canp.logans.me.uk`, BHPA links). Faster.
    *   Need: Site (name/grid ref), Date, Times (local), Activity, Altitude (AMSL), Contact.
*   **When:** Submit by 8pm evening before (Mon-Thurs), 3pm Fri for weekend/Mon.
*   **Tactics:** Can notify for launch site, or potential XC area downwind.
*   **Links:** [BHPA CANP Info](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/members/safety/canp/) , [CANP for Fliers App Info](https://www.bhpa.co.uk/members/safety/canp-for-free-fliers-app/)
