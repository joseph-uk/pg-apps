*   **What:** Civil Aircraft Notification Procedure.
*   **Purpose:** Notify primarily **midweek** air traffic (Military low flying, increasingly others via NOTAM) of planned flying activity.
*   **Why:** Safety! Makes low-flying aircraft aware of your potential presence. Creates temporary advisory area (2nm radius, up to 2000ft AGL).
*   **How:**
  *  use the CANP site built by Pete
*   **When:** Submit the evening before (Mon-Thurs), 3pm Fri for weekend/Mon.
  * Submit first thing in the morning and it often works as well
*   **Tactics:** Can notify for launch site, or potential XC area downwind.
*   **Link:** https://canp.logans.me.uk/
