*   **1. Lazy RASP:**
    *   Simplified web view for quick weekly overview (Stars, Wind).
    *   Good for spotting *potential* days. *Requires verification* with Windy.
    *   Link: https://lazyrasp.com/?p=stars
*   **2. Stratus RASP:**
    *   Most detailed RASP interface (best on desktop). For deep dives into all parameters. Steeper learning curve.
    *   Link: [http://rasp.stratus.org.uk/](http://rasp.stratus.org.uk/)
*   **3. XCPara RASP:**
    *   Mobile-friendly interface with Google Maps background. Simplified parameters. Good compromise for phone checks.
    *   Link: https://www.xcpara.co.uk/index.php/rasp-xcplanner
