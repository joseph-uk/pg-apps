*   **1. Ground Tracking (Persistent Location - For Retrieve):**
    *   **Recommended: Google Maps Location Sharing** (Reliable, integrates with nav, easy). Share with retrieve drivers / trusted buddies.
*   **2. Flight Tracking (Live Flight Data - For Flying):**
    *   **Apps (XCTrack/FlySkyHy):** Configure to upload to **XContest Live Tracking**.
    *   **PURETRACK (Aggregator):** The platform aiming to show *everyone* (XContest, FLARM, Satellite). Good place for spectators/retrieve to watch. 
      * Link: https://puretrack.io/
    *   **FLARM/OGN:** Dedicated devices for local awareness (seeing/being seen by gliders etc.). Important for busy airfields / alpine flying. Some integrated into varios (e.g., Skytraxx).
    *   **Satellite Trackers (InReach/SPOT):** Global coverage, feed into PURETRACK etc. Good for remote areas.
*   **Goal:** Be visible on Google Maps (ground) AND PURETRACK/XContest (air).
