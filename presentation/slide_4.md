*   **Overview:** Powerful, visual weather forecasting tool. Freemium model.
    *   Website: [https://www.windy.com](https://www.windy.com)
*   **Free vs. Premium:**
    *   Free: 3-hourly forecast intervals. Still very useful for initial checks.
    *   Premium (Highly Recommended): ~£2/month. *Primary benefit: 1-hourly forecast intervals.* Essential for detailed planning. Also more forecast updates, longer outlooks/loops. Well worth the cost (less than 1/3 tank of fuel/year!).
*   **Key Models:** Access multiple global and high-resolution models.
    *   **ECMWF:** High-quality European model (often default).
    *   **GFS:** US Global model (lower resolution, free, basis for RASP).
    *   **UKV:** UK Met Office high-resolution model (often most accurate for UK). **The go-to for detailed UK forecasts.**
    *   ICON, AROME, etc. available for comparison.
*   **Focus:** Use UKV primarily, check ECMWF/others for agreement = higher confidence.
