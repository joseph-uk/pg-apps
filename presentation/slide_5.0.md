*   **Layers:** Visualize key parameters: Wind (altitudes), Gusts, Clouds (base/levels), Rain, Thermals (CAPE/Lift), Temperature, Radar & Satellite etc. Crucial for understanding the day.
*   **Model Comparison View:** Side-by-side forecasts for a point/time. **Use this!** High agreement = more confidence. GFS disagreeing widely? Distrust GFS & RASP for that day.
*   **Soundings (Advanced):** Vertical atmospheric profile. Good for deeper dive into stability, inversion layers, thermal strength/height. More useful for experienced XC pilots.
*   **Point Forecasts:** Detailed meteograms/airgrams for specific sites.
*   **Webcams & Spots:** Integrated map for quick checks.
