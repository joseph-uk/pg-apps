*   **Context:** Aiming to fly XC. Even short XC flights benefit from planning.
*   **Why Plan?** Understand airspace, identify landing/retrieve options (roads, rail), have a goal, share route with others.
*   **Beginner XC:** Follow experienced pilots! Get *their* route QR code into your instrument. Provides a line, helps you follow, learn sensible routes.
*   **Tools:** Fly XC, Northern Challenge Series tasks, Open Railway Map.
