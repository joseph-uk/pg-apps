*   **What is RASP?** Regional Atmospheric Soaring Prediction.
    *   *Soaring specific* model enhancement, usually based on **GFS**.
*   **Characteristics:** Community-driven, complex, older tech. Unlikely to improve significantly.
*   **Key Limitation:** **Garbage In, Garbage Out.** If GFS forecast is wrong, RASP will be wrong. *Always cross-reference with Windy (UKV/ECMWF)!*
  * check the GFS forecast on windy, if it is wildly different to other models, ignore RASP forecast
*   **Common Use:** Quick look for "Star Rating" (XC potential 0-5) and Wind overview via simpler interfaces.
*   **Star Ratings:** 0 = likely unflyable/no lift, 5 = potential good XC day. Foot Launch stars are more conservative on wind.
