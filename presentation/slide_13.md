*   **1. Fly XC:**
    *   Web-based tool. Easy to use.
    *   Draw routes, view airspace, add waypoints, export (GPX, KML, instrument formats), **generate QR codes** for easy sharing via phone. **This is the go-to.**
    *   Link: [https://flyxc.app/](https://flyxc.app/)
*   **2. Northern Challenge Series:**
    *   Pre-defined tasks used in local friendly competitions.
    *   Excellent for practice even if not competing. Program into your instrument for navigation practice on the hill. Find tasks on NCS website/forums.
    *   Link: (Search for Northern Challenge Series Paragliding)
*   **3. Open Railway Map:**
    *   Map focused *only* on railway lines/stations. Quickly identify retrieve options near potential XC routes.
    *   Link: [https://www.openrailwaymap.org/](https://www.openrailwaymap.org/)
