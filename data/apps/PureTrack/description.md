# PureTrack: Comprehensive Paragliding Tracking Solution

PureTrack is a versatile tracking platform designed for paragliding and hang gliding enthusiasts. It integrates data from various tracking systems, offering real-time location sharing and enhanced situational awareness. Accessible via a web application, PureTrack supports numerous devices and apps, ensuring compatibility across different tracking methods.

## Key Features

- **Multi-Source Integration**:  
  PureTrack consolidates data from diverse tracking systems, including ADS-B, FLARM, OGN, SPOT, InReach satellite trackers, cellular apps, and dedicated cellular trackers. This integration provides comprehensive coverage and real-time tracking capabilities.  
  [Learn More](https://puretrack.io/help/comparison)

- **Device and App Compatibility**:  
  The platform supports a wide range of devices and applications such as XCSoar, XCTrack, SeeYou Navigator, FlySkyHy, Syride varios, Flymaster, and more. This ensures that pilots can utilize their preferred tools while benefiting from PureTrack's features.  
  [Supported Devices](https://puretrack.io/trackers)

- **Live Tracking and Sharing**:  
  Pilots can share their live location with friends and family through simple web links, enhancing safety and connectivity during flights. The platform also allows merging multiple tracking sources into a single map marker for streamlined monitoring.  
  [How It Works](https://puretrack.io/about/freeflight)

- **Situational Awareness**:  
  PureTrack offers features like live rain radar and the ability to view other aircraft in the vicinity, aiding pilots in making informed decisions during their flights.  
  [More Details](https://puretrack.io/about)

## Installation and Accessibility

PureTrack is primarily a web-based application, making it accessible on various devices without the need for dedicated apps. Users can install it on their smartphones or tablets by saving the website to their home screen. For iOS users, an official PureTrack app is available, which functions as a wrapper for the website and facilitates easy tracking.  
[Installation Guide](https://puretrack.io/help/faq)  
[Download iOS App](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/puretrack-gps-tracker/id6443511612)

## Subscription Options

- **Free Version**:  
  Offers essential safety tracking features, including unlimited tracking devices, 12-hour track history viewing, and IGC downloads.

- **PureTrack Pro**:  
  A premium subscription priced at $49 USD/year, providing extended features such as 6-day track history viewing, trip history for all aircraft, climb markers for gliders and paragliders, and advanced map options.  
  [Upgrade to Pro](https://puretrack.io/upgrade)
